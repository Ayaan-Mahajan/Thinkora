# Thinkora
**Think deeper. Decide better.**

Thinkora is a Python-based decision intelligence system designed to help people make thoughtful choices, reflect on outcomes, and better understand their own decision-making patterns over time.

Most decision tools answer a single question:

*"What should I choose?"*

Thinkora attempts to answer three more:

*Why did I choose it?*
*Did it work out?*
*What can I learn about myself from this decision?*

By combining structured evaluations, behavioral insights, reflections, and outcome tracking, Thinkora transforms decision-making into a process of self-discovery and continuous improvement.

## The Problem
People make hundreds of decisions every year, yet very few are documented.

Over time, we often forget:
- Why we chose a particular path
- Which factors influenced us most
- Whether the decision actually worked out
- What lessons we learned
- Which habits repeatedly shape our choices

As a result, we tend to repeat mistakes without fully understanding why.

Thinkora was built to preserve and learn from that journey.

## Why Thinkora Matters
Many productivity tools optimize actions.

Thinkora attempts to optimize judgment.

It encourages users to:
- Think deliberately
- Document reasoning
- Reflect consistently
- Learn from outcomes
- Recognize behavioral patterns
- Become more intentional decision-makers

Thinkora isn't designed to tell people what to do.

It's designed to help them become better at deciding for themselves.
